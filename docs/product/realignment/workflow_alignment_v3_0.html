5. Where the Act bites in this journey

Unchanged law (Amendment v2.4): one counting contact per customer per day across every channel; stating the amount is what makes an answered call count; an inconvenient-time call does not count and may be retried; inbound never counts; the slot is at stake for 90 seconds unless an amount is stated; the count locks at customer level so a substitute cannot re-contact; a Manager may override with a logged reason. Reminders before the due date are notifications, not contacts. The contact drawer states the cost on the option. The daily-slot law now runs on the contact log of REQ-COL-072, which is the only record; LINE is a channel.
