0. Plain language first

A test case here is a rule stated as something the system must do or refuse, with the code that proves it. e2e — a black-box check run against the live API by a script that never imports the code. Blind round — agents who play a first-time officer and may not read the source.

1. DAY - the daily loop

DAY-001 - A contact cannot be closed without next_action and next_action_on unless a PTP or service issue is created in the same call (e2e 25). DAY-002 - Today orders by the seven tiers in sequence; counts_by_tier reconcile to the rows; every row names its tier. DAY-003 - A PTP whose date passes is BROKEN by the system and broken_ptp_count increments; no endpoint sets the count. DAY-004 - The daily-slot law of v2.4 holds on the contact log: one counting contact per customer per day, inbound never counts, the 90 second retraction stands (e2e 13, 14 re-pointed). DAY-005 - A review request returns to the officer as a tier-6 item once answered; a second open request refuses. DAY-006 - payment_status and dc_status are independent: setting one never changes the other. DAY-007 - A power restriction request below the configured preconditions refuses; an approved one records the O&M notification with reason ARREARS; a confirmed payment sets restore_due_at by the 15:00 rule (e2e 26). DAY-008 - A service issue closure raises the tier-6 item and does not end the hold by itself. DAY-009 - Dictionaries refuse unknown codes and never hard-delete. DAY-010 - The role header gates review answers, NPL type, dictionary edits and assignment.

2. PIP - the invoice pipeline

PIP-001 - Release refuses while either gate is open (e2e 27). PIP-002 - Release stamps entity and company_id from the product group and creates an INVOICE_CREATE draft. PIP-003 - Delivery refuses until the document is APPROVED_IN_PEAK. PIP-004 - A reschedule requires one of three decisions; AMEND and CANCEL_AND_REISSUE create drafts; STAND creates nothing. PIP-005 - An unresolvable product group creates no document.

3. PKD - PEAK drafts

PKD-001 - The PEAK client exposes create_draft and read_state and nothing else; a reflection test fails if a third public method appears (e2e 28 asserts no approve route). PKD-002 - The idempotency key is persisted before the outbound call (a stub that fails mid-call still finds the key stored). PKD-003 - A replay with the same key returns the existing record and the stub records one create. PKD-004 - A transport failure leaves UNSENT and resend works. PKD-005 - A rejection carries the reason and raises a tier-6 item for the raiser. PKD-006 - The restructure raises two separate documents and either can be UNSENT alone. PKD-007 - Every one of the 23 intents maps to exactly one instance rule (EPC, SPV, both, or none) and the table in PEAK Integration v1.2 is the fixture. PKD-008 - A confirmed slip creates a draft receipt at invoice level and allocation still refuses (OD-02).

4. MIG - migration

MIG-001 - The importer reads every working tab and reconciles counts to the sheet's own pivots; a mismatch marks the run not reconciled (e2e 29). MIG-002 - MapLog strings become contact_log rows with timestamp, agent, result, PTP amount and date, next call. MIG-003 - Both date formats parse by tab; an unparseable date warns and creates no PTP. MIG-004 - Month-cell tokens map to month_events; Link Sent creates a delivery record, not a receipt. MIG-005 - The gap list contains exactly the orders installed per Odoo dates and not Order-Done. MIG-006 - Phone, email and identity numbers are masked on every imported row; the address mask defect of the first importer is fixed and asserted. MIG-007 - The sheet's late fee and the engine's late fee are both stored on the case during migration with the method named.

5. RPT - reports

RPT-001 - The weekly report reproduces the categories of the 2026-08 email and every ratio names its period. RPT-002 - Officer indicators are server-computed; no client test finds arithmetic in the interface.
