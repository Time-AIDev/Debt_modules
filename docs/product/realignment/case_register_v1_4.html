0. Plain language first

A case in this register is a situation someone will meet and the rule that answers it; it is not a customer case. An open item is a question the business has not yet answered, with the person who owns the answer.

1. New cases — D series (the daily loop)

CaseThe situationThe rule that answers it
DC1An officer logs a call and tries to close the drawer without saying what happens nextRefused (next_action_required). A PTP or a service issue in the same call sets the next action itself
DC2The customer says "call me back Thursday"next_action = CALLBACK on Thursday; the case appears in tier 2 on Thursday and is overdue on Friday
DC3A promise date passes with no receipt approved in PEAKThe system marks the PTP BROKEN, increments broken_ptp_count, and the case moves to tier 3; nobody types it
DC4Two officers reach the same customer on the same dayThe second attempt is refused by the daily-slot law (v2.4); the log shows who reached them first
DC5An officer wants the manager's view on one caseA review request with a reason and a question; the answer returns to the officer's Today list; a second open request on the same case refuses (review_pending)

DC8The Odoo ticket closes but the officer never hearsTier 6 item "ticket closed, resume follow-up" the next morning; the hold ends only when the officer confirms with the customer
DC9A Manager changes a bucket boundaryA new band set version; every bucket still travels with its band_set_code; history keeps the old set
DC10A case reads Late in payment_status and No Action in dc_statusBoth are true and both are shown; neither is derived from the other
