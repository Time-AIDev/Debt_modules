0. Plain language first

Entity here means a table or record shape, not the EPC/SPV company (which this document calls the legal entity, as v2.2 does). Append-only — rows are added, never changed. Dictionary — a governed list of allowed values.

1. New entities

case — uid, customer_uid, contract_uids, officer_uid (assignee), payment_status (CURRENT | LATE | NPL | CLOSED | NOT_INSTALLED, computed), dc_status (DC_STATUS dictionary, human), npl_type (NPL_TYPE dictionary, manager only), next_action (NEXT_ACTION dictionary), next_action_on, next_action_set_by, last_contact_at, last_contact_by, last_result, open_ptp_uid, broken_ptp_count, kept_ptp_count, renegotiation_count (all computed), open_service_issue_uid, power_state (ON | RESTRICTION_REQUESTED | RESTRICTED | RESTORE_DUE), review_request_uid, band pair (aging_bucket + band_set_code, v2.4 stands), entity + company_id (v2.2 stands), data_source, source_ref. Invariant: next_action and next_action_on are never null once the case is in collection.

contact_log — uid, case_uid, customer_uid, occurred_at, officer_uid, channel (PHONE | LINE | SMS | EMAIL | VISIT | INBOUND), direction (OUTBOUND | INBOUND), result (CONTACT_RESULT dictionary), amount_stated (bool), counted (bool, from the Act rule), note, ptp_uid, service_issue_uid, next_action, next_action_on, corrects_uid (a correction references the row it corrects), source (APP | MAPLOG_IMPORT | LINE_IMPORT). Append-only.

review_request — uid, case_uid, raised_by, raised_at, reason (REVIEW_REASON dictionary), question, status (OPEN | ANSWERED), answered_by, answered_at, decision (CONTINUE | ARRANGEMENT | RESTRUCTURE_PROPOSE | POWER_CUTOFF_APPROVE | NPL_TYPE_SET | LEGAL_REFER | OTHER), comment, sla_breached (computed). The system-raised manager_review of v2.2 stands and appears in the same list with raised_by = SYSTEM.

power_restriction — uid, case_uid, requested_by, requested_at, reason = ARREARS, missed_count_at_request, broken_ptp_at_request, approver_role, approved_by, approved_at, executed_by (O&M or officer), executed_at, notified_om_at, restore_due_at, restored_at, backfilled (true for the eighteen existing with no approver).

pipeline_item — uid, order_ref, customer_uid, product_group, entity + company_id (derived at release), planned_install_on, confirmed_install_on, actual_install_on, gate_down_payment (OPEN | PASSED with receipt or slip ref), gate_epc_confirmed (OPEN | PASSED with Odoo ref), released_at, released_by, invoice_uid, delivery {channel, sent_at, sent_by, reply}, install_outcome (INSTALLED | POSTPONED | CANCELLED), reschedule_decision (STAND | AMEND_DUE_DATE | CANCEL_AND_REISSUE), data_source.

peak_document — as PEAK Integration v1.2 section 2: uid, intent (23 values), entity + company_id, source_ref, payload, idempotency_key (stored before the call), state (PENDING | DRAFT_CREATED | APPROVED_IN_PEAK | REJECTED | UNSENT), peak_ref, peak_number, qr_payload, rejection_reason, created_by, created_at, state_read_at.

invoice (v2.2) gains peak_document_uid, and peak_state values become DRAFT_CREATED | APPROVED_IN_PEAK | REJECTED | UNSENT (SENT_PEAK retired); qr_payload is populated only from an approved document. receipt (v2.2) gains peak_document_uid and slip_ref (the confirmed slip); is_allocated stays false while OD-02 is open.

month_event — case_uid, contract_uid, month_index, kind (PAID amount | LATE | LINK_SENT | RESTRUCTURE | CREDIT_NOTE | NO_PAYMENT_SERVICE_ISSUE | CLOSED), amount, source_ref. The month grid on the case is drawn from this table so it looks exactly like the sheet; nothing computes money from it — money comes from the engine.

dictionary_value — dictionary (DC_STATUS | CONTACT_RESULT | NEXT_ACTION | REVIEW_REASON | NPL_TYPE | DELAY_CAUSE), code, label_th, label_en, sort_order, active, created_by, deactivated_at. Never hard-deleted.

band_set (v2.4) gains the seeded OPERATIONAL (1–29, 30–59, 60–89, 90+) and LEGACY_SHEET (under 30, 45, 60, 75, 90; over 90) definitions; a Manager may add a set; bucket key stays (code, band_set_code).

migration_row — import_run_uid, tab, row_no, volt_id, parsed (JSON), created_refs (case, contract, contact_log rows, month_events), warnings (date format, MapLog shift, unknown token), reconciled (bool). migration_run — uid, source_file, started_at, counts_by_tab, pivot_expected vs pivot_actual (per payment status and product), gap_list (orders installed per Odoo, not Order-Done).

weekly_report — period, cash_in_by_category {actual, target}, mom, wow_late, wow_npl with reasons, service_issue_count, next_week_focus (free text), generated_at, generated_by; every ratio carries its period.

2. Extended existing shapes

service_issue (v2.2) gains ticket_url, ticket_ref, ticket_status, responsible_team (INSTALLATION_EPC | O_AND_M | PERMIT | CS), deadline, closed_notified_at. ptp gains renegotiation_count and broken_detected_at (system-set). customer gains officer_uid (assignment at customer level) and line_search_key (the VOLT ID as used in LINE OA). working-list item priority_reason gains the seven tiers of REQ-COL-071 (PTP_DUE_TODAY | NEXT_ACTION_DUE | BROKEN_PTP | AT_RISK_TWO_MISSED | NEW_ARREARS | ANSWER_WAITING | ROUTINE).

3. Two traps restated

payment_status and dc_status are two fields and neither derives the other; a screen that shows one must show both. The month grid is a picture of history, not a ledger — arrears, DPD and fees come from the engine's instalment rows, and a migration warning is raised wherever the sheet's month cells and the engine disagree.
