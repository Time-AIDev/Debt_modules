0. How to read this record

A blind UAT round is run by testers who have never seen the app, playing the Debt team from the SOP and the seventeen-step workflow only. Every claim is re-run by an independent judge before it counts. Severity: P1 = wrong money on screen or a daily task that cannot be done; P2 = a step the tester could not finish unaided or finished wrongly because the screen misled them; P3 = polish. The stop rule (decision C7) is a full round with no P1, no P2 and at most three P3. Codes such as J-38 are the judge's finding ids; CF1, OD-02, OQ-44 are open business items in the Case Register.

2. What mattered most (the judge's plain words)

  1. The app could not tell an officer what a real customer owes or has paid: no invoice or receipt existed for the 151 sheet customers; a confirmed receipt did not show on the case; three figures were unexplained (3,199 on a 2,999 contract; sheet penalty 2,726 vs app none; 6,985.42 vs 7,215.83).
  2. Day one would still be two windows: call and LINE buttons dead on real customers because the fixture masks numbers (by design, production reads the sheet's numbers), and the morning list opened on everybody with four zero-balance June callbacks ranked above fourteen broken promises.
  3. Some governed acts said done when nothing happened: NPL type from the queue set nothing; a refused cut-off approval was stored and left the Owner's queue; restoring power left the customer marked as cut; a closed ticket stayed open on the case; the work-book export failed silently.

What worked and is now protected by tests: the mandatory next-action gate, the one-contact-per-day lock with its reason, promises and tickets setting the next action themselves, the officer → lead → officer review round trip, the two release gates and the no-link-before-AR-approves refusal, and the settings page naming which numbers are undecided and whose call they are.

4. P3 sweep

Thirty polish items were confirmed; all were fixed before round 2 (sixteen backend, twenty-three frontend, several overlapping). Highlights: the daily-contact refusal names the way through; every error shows one Thai sentence; the month grid opens on the current month; the invoice pipeline heading counts late, today and upcoming installs; a disabled release button says which gate is open; the payments screen says when PEAK is simulated; sidebar counts refresh after every act; the weekly report can be snapshotted; undecided settings are read-only below Owner; band sets can be deactivated but never deleted; the contact lock is per session.
